## Why this site exists

I've spent the last few years deep in robotics research — building multi-robot transport systems, training reinforcement learning policies for quadrotors, and developing computer vision pipelines at Sandia National Labs. Along the way, I've accumulated notes, debugging war stories, and opinions about how things should work.

This site is where those ideas will live.

## What to expect

I plan to write about:

- **Robotics engineering** — ROS workflows, SLAM debugging, control system design decisions, and the gap between simulation and reality.
- **Machine learning in practice** — Not just the theory, but the messy parts: hyperparameter tuning with Optuna, training on SLURM clusters, and getting PyTorch models to actually converge.
- **Computer vision** — RGBD pipelines, YOLO fine-tuning, real-time inference optimization with CUDA.
- **Tools and workflows** — Things I've found useful for research productivity, from Git workflows to experiment tracking with MLFlow.

## The stack

This site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and styled with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com). It's statically generated and deployed to GitHub Pages. Blog posts are written in Markdown and rendered at build time — no JavaScript frameworks, no client-side rendering, minimal overhead.

The design is inspired by Swiss-grid minimalism with a terminal aesthetic. Think Teenage Engineering meets Vercel.
